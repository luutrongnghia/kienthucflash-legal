Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · Operator: Nghia Luu (Kiến Thức Flash)

1. Overview

This policy describes how the Kiến Thức Flash self-hosted posting tool and this legal site handle information when you connect social accounts (such as TikTok) to schedule Shorts.

2. Data we process

  • Account identifiers and profile fields returned by the platform OAuth APIs (for example display name, avatar, open_id/username).
  • OAuth tokens needed to publish on your behalf, stored on your self-hosted server.
  • Media files and captions you upload for scheduling.
  • Basic technical logs (errors, request metadata) on the host machine.

3. How we use data

Data is used only to authenticate, schedule, and publish content you request, and to operate/debug the self-hosted instance. We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing

Content and required metadata are sent to the social platforms you connect (e.g. TikTok) according to their APIs and policies. Hosting providers you choose (for example GitHub Pages for this legal site) may process traffic logs.

5. Retention

Tokens and media remain on your self-hosted server until you delete them or disconnect the channel. You can revoke access in the platform’s app settings at any time.

6. Security

Use HTTPS in production, restrict server access, and rotate secrets if exposed. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

7. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required by local law).
